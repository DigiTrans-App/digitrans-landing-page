Evidence and human control

Approval follows the exact evidence reviewed.

01 / VERIFY THE SUPPORT

Make missing evidence visible

Checks use explicit policy and declared metadata: integrity, source authority and history, freshness, lifecycle, relevance, completeness, and independence.

Unresolved gaps, conflicting assertions, or a failed disclosure rule remain visible. Current checks do not infer truth from arbitrary prose.

02 / BIND THE APPROVAL

Keep the reviewer accountable

The approval binds the result, evidence snapshot, role, purpose, destination, conditions, and expiration. The reviewer must be authorized and separate from the roles the policy excludes.

Changes create a new review requirement. A previous approval cannot silently transfer to a changed subject or destination.

Execution and recovery

A lost response is an unresolved outcome.

An action may have completed even when the caller did not receive its response. Recovery must inspect durable records and preserve uncertainty until the outcome is established.

Completed

The outcome is recorded

A matching durable observation identifies the completed action. Recover the receipt and continue delivery of its recorded event.

Blocked

A required condition failed

Missing approval, changed scope, expired authority, or insufficient evidence prevents the action from proceeding.

Outcome uncertain

Further inspection is required

A timeout or missing lookup does not authorize another attempt. Escalate unresolved cases and retain the original action identity.

The synthetic references exercise concurrent requests, replay rejection, lost responses, process restart, and receipt tampering. Whole-system restore, distributed revocation, recovery time, and recovery point objectives still require deployment-specific evidence.
Portability architecture

Keep the control contract consistent. Validate the environment.

A different AI model and a different hosting platform are separate changes. Both need review of the exact capabilities used by the workflow.

MODEL CHOICE

Which model may assist?

Identify the approved provider, model, location, guardrails, evidence mapping, and permissions. Model access alone does not approve a workflow.

DEPLOYMENT PROFILE

Where do the controls operate?

Bind the application to its identity, storage, key custody, execution, event delivery, and recovery providers. Each adapter needs acceptance evidence.

SHARED CONTRACT

What must remain true?

Preserve tenant scope, independent approval, exact permitted use, minimized evidence, and visible failure or uncertainty across every environment.

AWS is the first deployment profile. Additional clouds and self-hosted production environments remain architecture work until their adapters and operating controls pass acceptance.
