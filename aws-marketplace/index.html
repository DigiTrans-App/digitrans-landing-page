Reduce procurement friction without weakening the assurance boundary.

Customer procurement sequence

Move from business problem to an accountable transaction.

A Marketplace listing does not replace qualification. The strongest path begins with one named problem, one accountable sponsor, one bounded pilot, and one agreed customer outcome.

01Identify the workflow

Select the AI use case, business consequence, review audience, and current governance boundary.

02Scope the pilot

Define two to three workflows, evidence requirements, stakeholders, duration, deliverables, and price.

03Confirm AWS alignment

Assess workload relevance, account-team participation, solution architecture, and incremental AWS value.

04Choose purchase options

Use the active listing and confirm any transaction-specific offer structure with DigiTrans.

05Execute and expand

Complete the pilot, validate outcomes, and define the production deployment and operating path.
