The healthcare AI trust challenge

Human oversight is essential. The evidence behind it must be reviewable.

Healthcare organizations already maintain meaningful controls around patient safety, privacy, clinical authority, quality, research, security, and compliance. As AI enters clinical, administrative, research, and patient-facing workflows, those controls need an operational record that shows how they worked in practice.

DigiTrust preserves the review path. It does not make clinical decisions, replace clinicians, provide legal advice, certify regulatory compliance, or override customer governance.

Priority workflows

Start where AI already influences care, research, communication, or operations.

01 / DOCUMENTATION

Ambient clinical documentation

Preserve source and workflow traceability, provider review, patient-choice evidence, corrections, retention controls, limitations, and note-generation history.

02 / RESEARCH

Clinical research and trial matching

Connect evidence source history, eligibility logic, recommendation context, human review, limitations, authorization, and reviewable later decisions.

03 / PATIENT

Patient communication and digital health

Record content and data source history, approved-use boundaries, personalization controls, human escalation, limitation disclosure, and accountability.

04 / OPERATIONS

Administrative and operational AI

Review scheduling, workflow automation, knowledge assistants, access and tool-use boundaries, approval, exception handling, and audit evidence.

05 / INNOVATION

Healthcare AI innovation programs

Preserve evaluation evidence, acceptance criteria, safety checks, deployment authority, known limitations, rollback decisions, and expansion evidence.

06 / CLINICAL SUPPORT

Clinical decision assistance

Connect source evidence, model or system context, checks, clinician authority, recommendation limits, exceptions, and the final accountable decision.

The DigiTrust healthcare trust record

Make existing human-review and governance controls operationally visible.

01 / EVIDENCE

Source and workflow history

Identify what information, systems, models, tools, and events influenced the AI-assisted result.

02 / VERIFY

Repeatable, rule-based checks

Record explicit integrity, quality, support, authority, contradiction, and disclosure controls.

03 / AUTHORITY

Clinician or accountable human approval

Bind the exact reviewer, role, scope, conditions, decision, and applicable time window.

04 / PURPOSE

Approved-use boundaries

Show whether the AI was used for the permitted purpose and stop changed or revoked use from inheriting approval.

05 / REVIEW

Known limits and review history

Keep uncertainty and unavailable evidence explicit and reproduce the history for governance, quality, privacy, security, or audit review.
