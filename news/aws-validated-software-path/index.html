Verified AWS milestones

  • AWS Partner Network Software Path — Validated stage

Why this matters to enterprise buyers

DigiTrust is designed for organizations that need AI-assisted work to remain reviewable, attributable, human-authorized, limitation-aware, and independently reconstructable. The AWS milestones provide additional technical and partner-channel credibility as DigiTrans begins controlled pilot and co-selling engagements.

Provider-neutral by design

Provider-specific integrations remain adapters around a reusable evidence, verification, approval, authorization, and audit core.
