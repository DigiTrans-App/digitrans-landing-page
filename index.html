The enterprise control gap

AI can act. Can your organization prove the action was valid?

Security tools show activity. Model monitoring shows behavior. Governance platforms define policy. Consequential AI also requires an independent operational record that binds evidence, verification, authority, exact use, limitations, and outcome.

The category

Enterprise AI Evidence and Assurance

DigiTrust creates one connected Trust Record across the controls that make consequential AI reviewable.

01 / EVIDENCE

Evidence and provenance

Preserve what the AI used, where it came from, its integrity, lifecycle, source context, and governed collection path.

02 / VERIFY

Deterministic verification

Apply explicit checks to evidence quality, claim support, authority, contradictions, disclosure, and bound inputs.

03 / AUTHORITY

Accountable human control

Bind decisions to the exact subject, authorized reviewer, scope, conditions, and time window.

04 / USE

Exact-use authorization

Permit only the approved subject and conditions. Changed, expired, revoked, or substituted use fails closed.

05 / AUDIT

Limitations and reconstruction

Keep hard findings visible, disclose what remains unavailable, and reproduce the full evidence and decision history.

Why DigiTrust

Assurance infrastructure—not another AI dashboard.

DigiTrust is designed to sit across models, agents, clouds, and enterprise systems while preserving a provider-neutral operational record.

01 / DETERMINISTICHard controls remain hard

Identity, integrity, authority, contradiction, disclosure, and use checks do not become optional because a model is persuasive.

02 / ACCOUNTABLEHumans retain authority

Agents cannot approve their own consequential output or transfer approval to a changed result.

03 / PORTABLEProvider-neutral by design

Canonical evidence and authority contracts remain reusable as clouds, models, and workflows change.

04 / EXPLICITLimitations stay visible

Unavailable evidence, unresolved findings, expired authority, and future capabilities are stated rather than hidden.

Current boundary: DigiTrust supports evidence and assurance workflows. It does not replace customer legal, clinical, security, audit, procurement, certification, or regulatory responsibility.
