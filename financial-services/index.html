The financial AI trust challenge

Strong governance still needs operational proof.

Financial institutions already maintain cybersecurity, model risk, compliance, audit, data governance, and third-party oversight programs. AI adds a new challenge: connecting the underlying data and workflow to clear checks, accountable decisions, approved use, known limits, and a record that can be reviewed later.

DigiTrust complements existing controls. It does not replace model risk management, information security, legal review, regulatory obligations, internal audit, or accountable business ownership.

Priority workflows

Start where the outcome already carries financial, customer, security, or regulatory consequence.

01 / FRAUD

Fraud and financial crime

Connect alerts, supporting information, investigation steps, recommended actions, analyst review, approval, exceptions, and final outcomes.

02 / RISK

Risk and decision support

Preserve source data, AI-system context, repeatable checks, human decision points, known limits, and a complete history of each recommendation.

03 / CUSTOMER

Customer intelligence and service

Record which customer signals influenced an outcome, what use was approved, when a person stepped in, and what was communicated.

04 / AGENTS

AI copilots and AI-agent operations

Track tools, data access, actions, approval steps, withdrawn approval, and the final accountable business outcome.

05 / CYBER

Cybersecurity AI

Connect alert evidence, triage recommendations, check results, security test findings, response approval, and incident review history.

06 / PAYMENTS

Payments and embedded finance

Make decisions across connected systems, identity checks, anti-money-laundering controls, fraud signals, payment records, approval boundaries, and exceptions easier to review.

The DigiTrust financial trust record

One connected record for evidence, checks, human approval, approved use, and review history.

01 / SOURCE

Evidence source history

Identify which data, documents, systems, tools, and events supported the AI-assisted outcome.

02 / CHECK

Repeatable, rule-based checks

Record clear checks for quality, integrity, supporting facts, approval rights, conflicts, and safe disclosure.

03 / DECIDE

Accountable human approval

Record the reviewer, role, approved purpose, conditions, decision, and time period.

04 / CONTROL

Approval for a specific use

Prevent a changed, expired, withdrawn, or substituted result from inheriting approval.

05 / RECONSTRUCT

Known limits and review history

Keep important concerns visible and preserve the path from evidence to the accountable outcome.
