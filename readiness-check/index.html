What happens next

DigiTrans follows up by work email to confirm the scope and arrange the initial review. The readiness check is complimentary. Any implementation, expanded assessment, or software subscription is scoped and priced separately before work begins.

The check supports planning; it does not certify compliance or replace legal, clinical, security, or audit review.
