A short walkthrough of your first review

  1. Open the directory entry. Select DigiTrust Evidence Assistant by DigiTrans LLC. Follow the available install or connect controls, then choose Try in chat. If your workspace restricts plugins, ask its administrator to enable access.
  2. Start with a synthetic example. Use the built-in demo below without supplying customer documents. For your own selected non-sensitive claims and excerpts, review the fields and confirm permission before sending them to DigiTrans.
  3. Inspect the result. Review claim-to-evidence links, missing details, quotation mismatches, supplied dates and reviewer questions. Use the findings to plan follow-up with the vendor and your review owner.

Know what the result establishes.

The review checks the material you select for structural gaps, exact quotation matches and supplied dates. It does not verify factual truth or source authenticity, certify compliance, approve a business decision or permit external sharing.

Keep credentials, personal information, patient records, payment data, private customer records and confidential documents out of the Assistant. Read the Assistant privacy policy before use.
