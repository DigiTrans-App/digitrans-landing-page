The category

Enterprise AI Evidence and Assurance

DigiTrust creates one connected Trust Record across the controls that make consequential AI reviewable.

01 / EVIDENCE

Evidence and source history

Preserve what the AI used, where it came from, whether it stayed intact, and how it moved through the workflow.

02 / VERIFY

Repeatable, rule-based checks

Use clear rules to check evidence quality, supporting facts, approval rights, conflicts, safe disclosure, and the exact information reviewed.

03 / AUTHORITY

Accountable human approval

Record who approved the result, what was approved, the conditions, and how long the approval lasts.

04 / USE

Approval for a specific use

Allow only the approved result and conditions. Changed, expired, withdrawn, or substituted use is blocked automatically.

05 / AUDIT

Known limits and complete review history

Keep important concerns visible, state what information is unavailable, and preserve the full evidence and decision history.

Why DigiTrust

A complete review record—not another AI dashboard.

DigiTrust works across AI models, agents, cloud platforms, and enterprise systems while preserving one consistent review record.

01 / REQUIRED CHECKSRequired checks cannot be bypassed

Identity, evidence integrity, approval rights, conflicts, safe disclosure, and approved-use checks cannot be skipped because an AI answer sounds persuasive.

02 / HUMAN APPROVALHumans retain authority

Agents cannot approve their own consequential output or transfer approval to a changed result.

The evidence and approval record remains useful even when cloud platforms, AI models, and workflows change.
