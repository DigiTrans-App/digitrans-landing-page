Why DigiTrust

Evidence requirements tied to approved use.

DigiTrust brings the evidence behind a specific business result into the same review path as its approval, permitted use, and recorded outcome. Start with one workflow and define what the result must satisfy before it is used.

01 / EVIDENCECheck support for the result

Review source references and declared freshness, completeness, and disclosure requirements. Keep missing support and conflicting evidence visible.

02 / APPROVALApprove this version for this use

Bind an independent reviewer to the exact result and evidence, purpose, destination, conditions, and time window. Changes require renewed review.

03 / REVIEW RECORDCarry the limits with the result

Keep the supporting sources, checks, corrections, and unresolved questions together so the next reviewer can understand the basis and limits of the decision.

Distinguish completed, blocked, and uncertain actions. Use the saved record to investigate a lost response before considering another attempt.

AI Evidence & Assurance · practical questions

Can you show how an AI-assisted decision was reviewed?

Start with a decision your team needs to make. Agree what evidence and human oversight are needed, then connect the review to its recorded outcome.

How can we demonstrate human oversight of an AI-assisted decision?

A useful review record connects the exact AI result to its supporting sources, checks, unresolved gaps, and an identified human reviewer's decision. DigiTrust brings those elements together with the approved purpose and recorded outcome, within the workflows agreed for an engagement.

What belongs in an AI decision audit trail?

Record the intended purpose and evidence requirements before use; the source material, result version, checks, and approval during review; and the outcome, limitations, and follow-up afterward. The record should make clear what is supported and what remains uncertain. A complete history supports review; it does not by itself establish that an AI result is correct.

How can AI and compliance teams agree on oversight?

Choose one workflow and agree on the evidence needed, the review owner, approval conditions, and how unresolved gaps will be handled. DigiTrans can scope a paid assessment, pilot, or implementation engagement around those requirements, with deliverables and success measures agreed with your team.

Can we explore the approach before sharing customer data?

Yes. The DigiTrust Evidence Assistant demo uses a fictional vendor review to show evidence links, findings, limitations, and the human decision still required. The public Assistant does not access private customer workspaces or authorize external use.

The category

AI Evidence & Assurance, from evidence to outcome.

Bring supporting sources, evidence checks, human approval, permitted use, and the recorded outcome into one review path. See how the controls work and what each deployment requires.

01 / EVIDENCE

Evidence and source history

Preserve what the AI used, where it came from, whether it stayed intact, and how it moved through the workflow.

02 / VERIFY

Repeatable, rule-based checks

Apply defined checks to declared source metadata, scope, disclosure, conflicts, and the exact information reviewed.

03 / AUTHORITY

Independent human approval

Bind a separate, authorized reviewer to the exact result, intended purpose, destination, conditions, and approval window.

04 / USE

Approval for a specific use

Record the intended purpose, destination, and approval window so reviewers can see where a result may be used.

05 / OUTCOME

Outcome and review history

See what completed, what was blocked, and what still needs investigation, alongside the supporting record.
